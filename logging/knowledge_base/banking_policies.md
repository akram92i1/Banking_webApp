# General Banking Policies and FAQs

## Account Types and Fees
### Standard Checking
- **Monthly Fee**: $0 (if minimum daily balance of $1,500 is maintained). Otherwise, $10.95/month.
- **Transactions**: Unlimited self-service transactions.
- **Interest**: None.

### Gold Checking
- **Monthly Fee**: $24.95/month (waived with $5,000 min balance).
- **Benefits**: 
  - Traceable World Elite Mastercard.
  - Free personalized cheques.
  - Annual fee rebate on select credit cards.

### Student Banking
- **Monthly Fee**: $0.
- **Eligibility**: Must be enrolled in a post-secondary institution.
- **Transactions**: Unlimited.

## Overdraft Protection
- **Standard Overdraft**: $5.00 per use + 21% interest per annum on overdrawn amount.
- **Overdraft Protection Plan**: $4.00/month flat fee + interest.

## Security & Fraud
### Lost or Stolen Card
- Immediately lock your card via the mobile app or call 1-800-BANK-SEC.
- Zero Liability policy applies for unauthorized transactions reported within 24 hours.

### Password Security
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Must contain: 1 Uppercase, 1 Lowercase, 1 Number, 1 Special Character.
- Never share your OTP (One-Time Password) with anyone, even bank staff.

## International Transfers
- **Wire Transfer**: 
  - Incoming: $15.00
  - Outgoing: $30.00 min (varies by country).
- **Processing Time**: 2-5 business days.
